    Curated Roadmaps: DSA vs CP

    Two distinct disciplines, two master trajectories. Choose the DSA Track for FAANG and product engineering interviews, or the Competitive Programming Track for Codeforces and ICPC.

    🎯 DSA Technical Interview Track

    • Target: Clear technical coding rounds at Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, Uber.
    • Time: 45-minute live interview loop with discussion, coding, and complexity proofs.
    • Bar: Clean variable naming, edge case handling, and communication clarity.
    • Core: Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Trees, Heaps, BFS/DFS, 1D/2D DP.

    ⚡ Competitive Programming Track

    • Target: High rating on Codeforces (Specialist/Master), CodeChef stars, ICPC qualification.
    • Time: 2-hour contests with strict penalty for wrong submissions.
    • Bar: Algorithmic correctness within $10^8$ ops/sec and 256MB memory.
    • Core: Number Theory, Segment Trees, Lazy Trees, Tree DP, Bitmask DP, Flows.
